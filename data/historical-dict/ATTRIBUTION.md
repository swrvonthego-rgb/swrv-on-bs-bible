# Historical Bible Dictionary — attribution

The definitions in `data/historical-dict/*.js` come from two public-domain
19th-century Bible dictionaries:

- **Easton's Bible Dictionary** (Matthew George Easton, 1897)
- **Smith's Bible Dictionary** (William Smith, 1863)

Both source dictionaries are in the public domain. The merged,
per-word JSON structure this app pulls from was compiled by NEUU
(`neuu-org/bible-dictionary-dataset` on GitHub) and is licensed
CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
This app credits that dataset here and in the in-app Sources/Library
listing, per the license's attribution requirement.
