Repent ≠ Confess

Two different Greek words. Repent (metanoeo) means to turn. Confess (homologeo) means to agree. The app shows the difference so you stop mixing them up.

Cultural Context Cards

Why did Adonijah grab the altar horns? Why did Solomon kill him for asking for a wife? Why was eating with sinners a scandal? Every passage with a hidden cultural meaning is explained from the ancient world — ANE law, Roman customs, first-century honor-shame culture.
