SWRV Kingdom Study Protocol — All 13 Rules

R01 The text is the authority. R02 Nothing added. Nothing removed. R03 Report what is written, not what is popular. R04 No opinions. Zero. R05 No whitewashing. R06 No outside sources — the library is closed. R07 No Greek philosophy. No Platonism. R08 No cherry-picking. R09 Always define the original word. R10 Always provide cultural and historical context. R11 Flag every translation loss. R12 Read through Ancient Near Eastern eyes. R13 If it cannot be sourced, it cannot be said.
