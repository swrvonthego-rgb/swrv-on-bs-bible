Privacy Policy

SWRV Kingdom Bible — operated by SWRV On The Go LLC · Atlanta, GA

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what the SWRV Kingdom Bible app ("the app") collects, why, and who it is shared with. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the features you choose to use. You can use the entire Bible, lexicons, search, and study tools without creating an account.

What we collect

  • If you create an account or sign in (optional): your email address, and — if you choose Google sign-in — the name and email your Google account shares with us. Sign-in and passwords (hashed, never stored in plain text) are handled entirely by our own servers on Cloudflare — we do not use a third-party identity or authentication provider.
  • Your study data (only when signed in): reading progress (which book, chapter, and verse you last read), personal notes you write, and bookmarks you save. These are stored in our own database so they can sync across your devices.
  • On-device settings: reading preferences, chosen voice, and recent searches are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Features that send data to third parties

  • Read-aloud (text-to-speech): when you tap play, the verse text being read is sent to ElevenLabs to generate audio. It is used only to return the spoken audio.
  • AI study features: if you use an AI-assisted feature, the text of your request is relayed to Groq to generate a response.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us to create your account.
  • Hosting & fonts: the app, your account, and your data are all served and stored by Cloudflare; web fonts load from Google Fonts, which by nature receives your device's IP address to deliver those files.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above, solely to deliver the feature you used.

Data retention & your control

  • Your notes, bookmarks, and progress remain until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • You can sign out at any time; on-device data can be cleared by removing the app or clearing its storage.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, email contact@swrvonthego.pro and we will remove it.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this address.
